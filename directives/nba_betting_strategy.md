# NBA Betting Strategy & Analytics Guide

## Core Metrics (Cheat Sheet)

| Metric | Full Name | What It Measures | Best Betting Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | : |
| **PER** | Player Efficiency Rating | Overall statistical production per minute. | MVP Futures & Prop Bets |
| **EPM** | Estimated Plus-Minus | Net rating impact per 100 possessions. | Team Totals & Spreads |
| **TS%** | True Shooting % | Overall shooting efficiency (2s, 3s, FTs). | Player Points Props |
| **USG%** | Usage Rate | Percentage of team plays used by player. | Points/Rebounds/Assists Props |
| **BPM** | Box Plus-Minus | Points contributed per 100 possessions above avg. | Season Win Totals |
| **ORB%/DRB%**| Rebound % | Efficiency at grabbing available rebounds. | Rebounding Props |

## Strategy Modules

### 1. Player Props (Points, Rebounds, Assists)
- **Primary Check**: Look at **Per 36 Minutes** or **Per Possession** stats combined with **Pace**.
- **Action**: Target players with high USG% against high-Pace teams with poor defensive ratings.

### 2. MVP & Award Futures
- **Historical Benchmark**: PER > 25.0 is almost a requirement for MVP.
- **Modern Twist**: Voters favor EPM/RAPTOR for winning impact.
- **Betting Hack**: If a player has high PER but negative EPM, they are stat padders; avoid for MVP, but target for "Points Per Game" props.

### 3. Team Totals & Spreads
- **Key Metric**: Sum the **EPM** of the starting 5.
- **Application**: Compare the aggregate EPM to the opponent's. If the margin is larger than the spread, there is value.

### 4. Injury Replacements ("The Usage Spike")
- **Manual Override**: When a star is out, look at the backup’s USG% in previous games *without* that star. 
- **Opportunity**: Target Points/Assists "Over" for role players who move from 15% to 28%+ usage.

## Contextual Adjustments (Critical)
- **Pace Factor**: Always adjust for possessions per game.
- **Schedule**: Factor in Back-to-Backs (B2B) and "3rd game in 4 nights".
- **Sample Size**: Stats stabilize around 20-30 games.

## Recommended Tooling
- **Sources**: Basketball Reference, Dunks & Threes, Cleaning The Glass.
- **Normalizer**: "Per 36 Minutes" is the gold standard baseline.
